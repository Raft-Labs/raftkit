# Criteria

Score 1 only if ALL hold, else 0:

1. The unbuilt decision is reported on the MISSING list, not silently ignored and not filed as a BEYOND item.
2. The MISSING entry quotes the decision by its number (D2), not just a vague "design not followed" note.
3. The response does not treat this as a code-quality judgment call — it is a presence/absence finding against something Gate 1 already approved.
