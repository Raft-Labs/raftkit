# Criteria

Score 1 only if ALL hold, else 0:

1. Only the guest criterion is listed as MISSING; the two covered criteria are
   not.
2. The report says the PR is blocked until the guest criterion is built or
   explained, and that a wrong story goes back to the PM through a story amend.
3. Nothing is listed as BEYOND THE STORY.
