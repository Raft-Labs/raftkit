# Extraction and citations

## Three categories, nothing else

- **Decisions** — a choice the call settled, not an open option or a discussion.
- **Scope changes** — anything the client asked for beyond the current SOW or Profile scope. Never folded into the plan as agreed work.
- **Action items** — a concrete next step someone owns. These become the task batch.

Small talk, rehash and asides are not extracted.

## One citation form

```output
<meeting> @ <timestamp>
```

`<meeting>` is the recording title or share link; `<timestamp>` is the deep link into the transcript at that moment, returned when the recording URL is passed to the transcript call. An uploaded transcript cites its own inline timestamps and names itself "uploaded transcript, as-of <date>"; never manufacture a link for it.

## The SCOPE CHANGE flag

```output
SCOPE CHANGE — <what the client asked for>  [<meeting> @ <timestamp>]
  Routing: PM handles / escalate to founders if commercial
```

Commercial means budget, contract, relationship risk or a client commitment such as a date. A scope change may also be an action item ("PM to quote the extra module") as long as it stays flagged.

## Long transcripts

Extract per chunk, merge the three lists, deduplicate items spanning a boundary. A half-read call yields false-empty results; read all of it.

## Recording errors

Not found (bad link or name) → say the identifier is wrong. No access → say the connector cannot reach the recording and to grant access. Never guess which meeting was meant.
