# Criteria

The countable rules are this case's regex graders. Score 1 only if ALL of
these hold, else 0:

1. The thin week is stated plainly as a thin week (e.g. "quiet week — no
   shipped items") — it is not padded with vague filler to sound busier than
   the board shows, and it does not invent progress not on the board.
2. The update ends with exactly one clear ask — the carried-over payment
   provider decision — not zero asks and not several competing ones.
3. The skill does not claim to have sent the draft or offer to send it.
4. No opening throat-clearing before the draft.
5. A reader with no Asana or RaftKit background could read the draft once and
   know exactly what to decide.
6. A term a first-time reader could not follow is explained in a few words on
   first use; shared terms the team uses daily are used as they are, not given
   an invented definition.
